FAQ

Quick answers.

Still have a question? Write to SATMakon on Telegram.

@sat_makon

Is MakonBook free?

Yes. Create a free account with your email: practice tests, Mistakes, vocabulary, the Universities catalogue and the first Admissions lessons are open. Current SATMakon students get everything.

I study at SATMakon. How do I get full access?

Sign up with your email, choose “I study at SATMakon” and confirm the phone number SATMakon has for you with an SMS code. Your classrooms, homework and the full library open at once.

How do I check my level?

Take the free level check: Reading & Writing and Math, with a separate score for each subject. No account needed.

Do I need an account for mock exams?

No. Register for a seat with your email. SATMakon students can register with their MakonBook login.

What can parents see?

Attendance, lesson ratings, test results and homework, opened with the student's registered phone number.

Are the results on this page real?

Yes: they are SATMakon student results that SATMakon has published.
