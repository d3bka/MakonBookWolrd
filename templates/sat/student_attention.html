How students get on this list

A student is listed for one strong reason (two missed homework, two missed lessons, a lower score) or two weaker ones together (one missed homework and no visits, for example).

  • Homework past its deadline and not done, or marked “Not done” by the teacher, in the last {{ rules.window }} days.
  • Homework marked “Partly done” two or more times.
  • Two or more missed lessons in the last {{ rules.window }} days (attendance from Hollihop).
  • The last test score is clearly below the student’s usual score for the same kind of test.
  • No visit to MakonBook for {{ rules.inactive }} days or more.
